## Current Sub-processors

Since Bitrix24 Enterprise On-Premise runs on JOL's own infrastructure, there are
currently **no external sub-processors** involved in CRM data processing.

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location | DPA Status |
|---------------|---------|----------|------------|
| *(none)* | — | — | — |

## Infrastructure Components (JOL-operated)

These are JOL-operated infrastructure components, not sub-processors:

| Component | Purpose | Location |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Proxmox VE cluster | Virtualisation host | EU (JOL data centre) |
| PostgreSQL database | Sync state storage | EU (JOL data centre) |
| Bitrix24 Enterprise | CRM application | EU (JOL data centre) |

## Notification Process

Any future sub-processor additions will be documented here and notified to
affected data subjects with 30 days' advance notice, as required by GDPR Art. 28(2).
